# Fresha: Online booking, Marketing, Team, Reports, Add-ons, Settings (captured 18 Oct 2026)

Captured two days before the Fresha trial ended. Copy is paraphrased; Bookly must use its own wording.
Shared patterns: "INCLUDED IN YOUR PLAN" feature-intro pages (green eyebrow, big title, ticked bullets,
black "Start now" pill + "Learn more", illustration on the right), list pages with a count bubble next to
the title, Options (white) + Add (black) pills top right.

## Online booking
- **Marketplace profile** (/fresha/online-booking/locations): intro page (attract new clients, list on the
  marketplace app, clients self-book 24/7). Start now.
- **Reserve with Google**: redirects to Add-ons.
- **Facebook and Instagram bookings**: intro (free bookings for new and returning clients, connect pages,
  promote a Book Now button). "Set up now".
- **Link builder** (/buttons-and-links): title + "Create shareable links and QR codes for anything that your
  clients can book or buy online." Two cards:
  - Link to everything: one link for everything bookable. Create link (needs a published profile, otherwise
    a full-screen dark modal asks to publish the profile first).
  - Link to services: links for certain services, locations or team members. Create link.
- **Smart Website**: paid add-on; page redirects to Marketplace profile on trial.

  - Detail page (/overview/appointment-reminder-1): Back, breadcrumb "Automations · Appointment reminder", title,
    Enabled badge, Options; tabs Performance (Year to date; sent-messages chart by Email/WhatsApp; Sent, Delivered,

    appointment; Channels: Email, WhatsApp; Created/Updated dates).
- **Messages history**: intro until messages are sent.

## Team
- **Team members** (/team/team-members): title + count bubble; Options (Create share link, Change order, Team settings |
  Export CSV, Excel); Add → full-screen "Add team member". Search, Filters, Custom order. Table: Name (avatar),
  Contact (email, phone), Permission role (Workspace owner / No access), Actions (Edit, View calendar, View scheduled
  shifts, Add time off).
  - Add team member sections: Profile, Addresses, Emergency contacts, Services, Locations | Settings, Wages and
    timesheets, Commissions, Pay runs. Profile: First name*, Last name, Email*, Phone (+country code), Additional
    phone, Country, Birthday (day + month), Gender (Female/Male/Non-binary/Prefer not to say), Pronouns
    (She/Her, He/Him, They/Them, Prefer not to say), Calendar color, Job title, start date, employment type…
- **Scheduled shifts** (/team/scheduled-shifts?date=): Options (Scheduling settings); Add (Time off, New team member,
  Business closed period). Toolbar: sort, ‹ week range › . Grid: rows = team members (avatar, name, weekly hours,
  pencil), columns = days with daily total hours; purple shift chips "10:00 - 19:00"; "Not working".
  - Shift chip menu: Edit this day, Set repeating shifts, Add time off, Delete this shift (red).
  - Member pencil menu: Schedule — Set repeating shifts, Unassign from location, Delete all shifts (red);
    Team member — View team member, Edit team member.
  - Set repeating shifts (full screen, Close/Save): "Set {name}'s repeating shifts"; location card; Schedule type
    (Every week / 2 / 3 / 4 weeks); Start date; Ends (Never / Specific date); note that closed periods are skipped;
    Weekly list Mon–Sun with hours and "10:00 - 19:00" + Edit, total hours.
  - Footer note: roster is separate from business opening hours (link to set them).
- **Timesheets**, **Pay runs**: intro pages.

    Liability summary/activity, Prepayments by time period*, Prepayment list, Taxes list.

    Waitlist summary*.
  - Team: Working hours activity, Break activity, Attendance summary, Wages detail/summary, Fee deduction

- Report page (/reports/table/sales-summary): Back, breadcrumb, title + favourite star, description + "Data from

## Settings (/setup) "Workspace settings"
Tabs: Settings, Online presence, Marketing, Other. Cards:
- Business setup → Business details, Locations, Service menu, Product list, Memberships, Packages, Clients list.
  (Business details: name, country, currency, tax calculation, team/client default language, external links
  Facebook/X/Instagram/Website.)
- Scheduling → Time and calendar, Waitlist appointments, Blocked time types, Resources, Cancellation reasons,
  Appointment statuses, Closed periods | Online booking: Dynamic assignment, Availability, Booking options,
  Marketplace profile, Service menu, Scheduled shifts. (Time and calendar: time zone, 24-hour format, first day of
  week; calendar settings: appointment colour source = Category, display processing time, display blocked time.)
- Sales → Pay now, Tax rates, Receipts, Registers, Tipping, Service charges, Gift cards, Custom checkout methods.
- Clients → Client sources, Client tags, Client Connect.
- Billing → invoices, text messages, add-ons.
- Team → Permission roles, Time off types, Timesheets, Shifts, Pay runs, Commissions, PIN switching.
- Forms → Form templates (Add; template rows with Inactive badge + Actions).
- Payments → Payment policy, Payment methods, Card terminals.
Online presence / Marketing / Other tabs repeat the cards above with "View" links.
